Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Claude MCP is a personal-use application operated by Doug Ellsley for his own Google account only.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app accesses the owner's Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, Gmail and Calendar data in order to carry out requests the owner makes.

Data collection, storage and sharing

The app runs on the owner's own computer. It does not collect data from anyone else, does not send Google user data to any server operated by the developer, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties. The only data stored is an authorization token kept on the owner's computer, which the owner can revoke at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
